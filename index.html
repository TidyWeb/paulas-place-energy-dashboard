04 / YOUR DIY BUDGET

About £5,000, all in.

Assumed · Phil’s estimate · editable

You are selecting the components and fitting the system yourselves. This is your total DIY capital estimate, including your chosen components and fitting costs.

One all-in figure. No contractor installation allowances or example-kit prices are added to it. Adjust the total if your chosen specification changes. Any heat-pump capital cost is included only if you allow for it in this total.

No grant is deducted by default. Enter only the contribution for the measures you are considering; confirm eligibility and avoid combining incompatible awards.

Simple payback compares your DIY budget with avoided electricity costs and export income, using the same household demand on Flux without PV/storage as the baseline. It excludes finance, maintenance, replacements, degradation and gas savings. With a heat pump on, this is not whole-heating-system payback: avoided fuel bills and a heat-loss assessment are missing.

Where the numbers come from

Solar generation uses the saved PVGIS 1 kWp hourly profile for 2021–2023. It was downloaded at an older postcode point, with an optimum south-facing array. The front roof’s provisional southeast aspect is a screening adjustment, not a fresh PVGIS roof simulation.

“Measured/derived” here means calculated, not measured at the property. Inputs marked “Imported from project data” can themselves be modelled data or earlier assumptions. Costs are editable placeholders. No carbon figure is shown because no agreed emissions factor or counterfactual has been supplied.
