Fine-tune & small print
  • Built from 2021–2023 hourly solar data (PVGIS) and Octopus Flux prices from 15 September 2026. Prices change.
  • The front roof fits about 6 panels (size guessed from the sale listing). Any more go on the back / ground area, which is treated as big enough. Nobody has measured it yet, and there's no shading survey.
  • With the standard 3.68 kW export limit, sunny-day power that the house and battery can’t take is wasted. From about 14 panels upwards, that loss grows quickly: roughly 665 kWh a year at 20 panels. The 8 kW inverter itself only starts capping output beyond about 24 panels.
  • Bills include the 68p/day standing charge. Savings compare the same house on Flux with no panels.
  • Night charging and peak selling assume the system knows tomorrow’s weather perfectly, so they’re on the hopeful side.
  • Peak selling keeps enough in the battery to run the house until 2am. Selling is capped by block 7: about 11 kWh over the 3 hours at 3.68 kW. At 8 kW, the battery’s assumed 5 kW output becomes the cap, at about 15 kWh.
  • Heat pump savings on gas aren’t counted. Payback ignores repairs, finance and panel ageing.
  • A planning guide, not a quote or an engineering design.
